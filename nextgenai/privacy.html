Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 9, 2026

1. Introduction

NextGenAI ("we", "us") handles personal information responsibly. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and protect information when you visit our website, contact us, create an account, or use our products: AI Business Manager, Cineforge AI, Contractor AI and related services.

2. Information We Collect

Personal information you provide: name, email, phone number, company details, and anything you submit through contact, support or investor forms. Account information: identifiers, login and authentication details, preferences and subscription data. We do not intentionally store plain-text passwords. AI inputs and outputs: prompts, documents, images, business information and video instructions you submit, plus generated outputs, usage records and error reports. Please do not submit confidential or sensitive information unless it is necessary and you are authorized to share it. Technical data: IP address, browser and device, pages visited, timestamps, approximate location and diagnostic logs.

3. How We Use Information

To provide and operate our website and AI products, manage accounts, process prompts and deliver outputs, answer inquiries and investor communications, process subscriptions and payments, monitor performance and security, detect fraud and abuse, improve our products, send essential service messages, send marketing where permitted by law (with consent when required), and meet legal obligations.

4. AI Services and Third-Party Providers

Some features rely on third-party providers for AI models, hosting, authentication, analytics, communications, social media integrations and video generation. Relevant information may be sent to them to deliver the feature, and they handle it under their own policies. We cannot guarantee the practices of independent third parties. Review a provider's terms before submitting confidential information.

5. Social Media and Connected Accounts

If you connect a social media or other account, we access only the permissions needed for the features you select, such as preparing or publishing content. You can revoke access in the platform's settings or in our product where available; revoking may disable related features.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies to maintain sessions, remember preferences, support essential functions, understand usage and improve security. Where law requires, non-essential technologies are used only with consent. You can manage cookies in your browser settings.

7. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share it with service providers that support our operations, with connected services you authorize, when required by law or valid legal process, to investigate fraud, abuse or security incidents, in a merger, financing or sale of business assets, or with your consent. We limit disclosures to what is reasonably necessary.

8. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain accounts, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, maintain security and meet legal duties. We then delete, anonymize or securely dispose of it. Deleted data may remain briefly in backups or with third-party providers under their retention policies.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures such as access controls, authentication, encrypted connections and monitoring. No system is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your credentials confidential and telling us if you suspect unauthorized access.

10. International Data Transfers

Our providers and infrastructure may process information in countries other than yours. Where transfers occur we take steps required by applicable law to protect the information; protections may vary by location.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your information, request a portable copy, withdraw consent, opt out of marketing, and complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to exercise these rights. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where the law permits or requires.

12. Children's Privacy

Our website and products are not intended for children below the age at which they may consent to data processing under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect their information without required parental consent. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will review it.

13. Third-Party Websites

Our site may link to third-party sites and services we do not control. Their content and privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

14. Marketing Communications

You can unsubscribe from marketing using the link in a message or by contacting us. We may still send essential messages about your account, transactions, security or requested services.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services, technology or legal obligations. We will update the date above and, where required by law, give additional notice or obtain consent for material changes.
